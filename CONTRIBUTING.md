# Contributing to Berean

Welcome. Berean is built by a community of people who love the Word and want to
study it honestly. There's a place here whether you write code, know the
languages, love the history, or simply study faithfully.

## Ways to help

- **Texts & data** — help ingest public-domain translations and original-language
  texts into the corpus (see [docs/03-texts-and-licensing.md](docs/03-texts-and-licensing.md) first).
- **Scholarship & evidence** — contribute sourced manuscript, textual-criticism,
  and historical/archaeological material for the Evidence pillar.
- **Tools & code** — the Library reader, Sermon Studio, Church Operations.
- **Languages (i18n)** — help make Berean usable in more tongues.
- **Study & feedback** — use it, report what's confusing or wrong, request features.

## The Berean rule (please read)

1. **Cite your sources.** Every historical, textual, or scholarly claim carries a
   reference. We help readers *examine*, as the Bereans did — so we hold ourselves
   to the same standard. No unsourced assertions.
2. **Respect the licenses.** Never add a copyrighted translation or work to the
   repo. Public-domain or openly-licensed texts only; everything else is linked or
   accessed via a licensed API. Record every text in the manifest.
3. **Be non-sectarian.** Traditions differ on canon, translation, and
   interpretation. Present the differences fairly and label them; don't smuggle in
   one tradition as "the" answer. Contributions are welcome from every tradition.
4. **Handle the text with reverence and accuracy.** Care with Scripture is not
   optional here.
5. **Be gracious.** Disagree about interpretation kindly. This is a community of
   students, not a battlefield.

## How to propose a change

Open an issue describing the idea (or the source you want to add) before large
work, so we can agree on scope and licensing. Keep pull requests focused. For text
additions, include the source edition, year, language, tradition/canon status, and
license in the manifest entry.

Thank you for helping people search the Scriptures.
